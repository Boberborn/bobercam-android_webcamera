# BobrCam — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 2026-08-20*

BobrCam lets you use your phone's camera as a webcam by streaming video to the BobrCam app on your Windows PC.

## What data BobrCam accesses

- **Camera video and (if enabled) audio** from your phone. This is the core function of the app.

## How that data is used

- The camera/audio stream is processed and transmitted **only over your own local network**, directly between your phone and your paired Windows PC.
- The Windows app receives the stream to display it and/or expose it as a virtual camera device for other applications on that PC.

## What BobrCam does NOT do

- It does **not** upload, store, record, or transmit your camera or microphone data to any server, the developer, or any third party.
- It does **not** collect contacts, location, account information, or any other personal data.
- It does **not** use analytics, advertising, or telemetry of any kind.

## Data retention

- No video, audio, or personal data is retained by BobrCam. Streams exist only in memory while the app is actively streaming and are discarded when you stop.

## Your control

- You can stop streaming at any time, which immediately ends all data transmission.
- Uninstalling the app removes it completely from your device.
